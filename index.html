Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with NexaPoint BPO.

How do your callers source prospective facility data?

+
We build custom, proprietary B2B contact lists targeting commercial office towers, healthcare facilities, warehouses, and schools within your designated postal codes. Every record is scrubbed against National Do Not Call (DNC) registers and phone numbers are pre-verified before dialing.

Can your reps work directly inside our CRM?

+
Yes. Our representatives work seamlessly with Jobber, Housecall Pro, Swept, CleanGuru, and HubSpot. When a walkthrough is confirmed, the rep creates the opportunity, logs building square footage, and syncs calendar invites directly to your estimator.

Do you display local phone numbers when calling?

+
Yes. We provision local geographic caller IDs for every target market (e.g., Manchester 0161 in the UK, Toronto 416 in Canada, Auckland 09 in NZ, San Antonio 210 in the US), ensuring high connection and answer rates.

What are your agreement terms?

+
We begin with an initial 60-day campaign calibration period to establish prospect lists, refine scripts, and stabilize appointment volume. After 60 days, agreements convert to flexible month-to-month contracts.
