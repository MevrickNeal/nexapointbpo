Direct Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with NexaPoint BPO.

How do your callers get the local building & decision-maker data?

+
We build custom, proprietary B2B data lists specifically for your target postal codes and service areas using enterprise data providers (ZoomInfo, Apollo, Google Maps scrapers, local business registries). Every record is scrubbed against National Do Not Call (DNC) lists and phone numbers are pre-verified before dialing.

Can your reps integrate with our existing CRM (Jobber, Housecall Pro, HubSpot)?

+
Yes! Our team works seamlessly inside your CRM. When a walkthrough is confirmed, the rep creates the lead card, adds the square footage details, notes current pain points, and invites your estimator directly on your calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook, or Calendly).

How do you ensure cold callers don't burn my cleaning brand's reputation?

+
We never use high-pressure, aggressive, or spammy tactics. Our approach is consultative and professional. We inquire about their existing cleaning satisfaction, contract renewal dates, and offer a zero-obligation walkthrough comparison. 100% of our booked calls are QA audited by supervisors before confirmation.

Are there long-term contracts or locked-in commitments?

+
We offer flexible month-to-month agreements after an initial 60-day campaign calibration period. Our philosophy is simple: *"Your Goals, Our Support."* We retain our cleaning partners because of tangible ROI, not restrictive contracts.
