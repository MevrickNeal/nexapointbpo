Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers to every question our prospective clients ask before onboarding.

How do you source decision-maker data for our target area?

+
We build custom B2B prospect lists for your specific postcodes using enterprise databases (Apollo.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Google Maps), local business registers and planning portals. Every number is verified and scrubbed against the relevant national Do Not Call registry before we dial.

Will your callers work directly inside our CRM (Jobber, HubSpot, Housecall Pro)?

+
Yes. Our agents receive full CRM training for your platform. When a walkthrough is confirmed, the agent creates the lead record, attaches building notes, decision-maker contact details and current vendor pain points, and sends a calendar invite to your estimator — all within the same call.

How do you ensure our brand is represented professionally?

+
Every agent undergoes accent clarity and commercial janitorial industry training before being assigned to any client. All walkthrough-confirmation calls are audited by QA supervisors and scored against a 20-point rubric. Any call scoring below 70% triggers immediate retraining. You also receive access to your private call recording portal so you can listen in at any time.

What is the minimum commitment period?

+
We require an initial 60-day calibration period to build your prospect list, refine scripts, and establish reliable walkthrough volume. After that, all agreements are month-to-month. We retain clients through results, not restrictive contracts.

Do your agents use a local phone number so prospects see a local caller ID?

+
Yes. We provision local geographic phone numbers matching your target city — for example, a Manchester 0161 number for UK campaigns, a 416 Toronto number for Canadian campaigns, or an Auckland 09 number for New Zealand campaigns. This significantly improves answer rates compared to international or unknown numbers.

How many walkthroughs can we realistically expect each month?

+
Based on our current active campaigns, one dedicated full-time caller books between 12 and 18 confirmed commercial cleaning walkthroughs per month. This varies depending on your target facility size, local market density and the quality of your existing pitch and credibility materials. We provide conservative projections during your discovery consultation.
